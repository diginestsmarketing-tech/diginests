FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Social Media Marketing in Shimla

How often will you shoot content at my hotel/business in Shimla?

We schedule dedicated monthly or bi-weekly on-site content creation days across Shimla and nearby areas to shoot fresh Reels, photos and drone footage.

Do you run Facebook and Instagram Ads, or just post organically?

We manage both organic content (Reels, Grid posts, Stories) and paid Meta Ad campaigns to guarantee measurable lead growth and bookings.

Can social media marketing replace OTAs for my stay?

Social media builds direct brand equity. Combined with Meta Click-to-WhatsApp ads, it can significantly increase direct bookings and lower your dependence on OTA commissions.

Who writes the captions and scripts for Instagram Reels?

DigiNests handles end-to-end content creation, including scripting, trend research, caption writing, hashtag strategy and scheduling.

How do you measure ROI from social media management?

We track Instagram reach, Reel views, DM booking inquiries, WhatsApp lead clicks and ad Cost Per Lead (CPL) in a transparent monthly dashboard.

What social platforms do you manage?

We primarily focus on Instagram and Facebook, where local visual content performs best in Himachal, alongside YouTube Shorts syndication.
