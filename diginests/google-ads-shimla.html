FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions About Google Ads in Shimla

How much ad budget do I need to start running Google Ads in Shimla?

We recommend starting with ₹15,000–₹30,000 per month in ad spend. This is enough to gather meaningful data on local searches, and we scale up once leads and ROAS are proven.

How quickly will I start getting phone calls or inquiries?

Most campaigns begin generating calls and enquiries within 24–48 hours of launch. Ongoing optimization then improves lead quality and lowers your cost per lead.

Can Google Ads help my hotel bypass high OTA commissions?

Yes. Direct search ads and Google Hotel Ads put your own booking link in front of travelers, so more guests book with you directly instead of paying 15–20% to OTAs.

Why hire DigiNests instead of running ads myself?

Self-managed local campaigns often lose over half their budget to broad keywords and untracked clicks. We shield your spend with negative keywords, exact-intent targeting and conversion tracking.

How do you track conversions and prove ROI?

We set up call tracking, form tracking and WhatsApp click tracking, then share monthly dashboards showing cost per lead (CPL), CPA and ROAS, so you see what every rupee returned.

Do I need a custom landing page for Google Ads?

Yes. A custom page matched to each ad group lifts your Quality Score, lowers your cost per click, and prevents wasted spend on visitors who land on a generic homepage.
